Meri jaan Bhumika,

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki tumhari tareef kahan se shuru karun, kyunki tum mein pasand karne ko sirf ek baat nahi—ek poora khoobsurat jahaan hai. Tumhari muskaan mere din ko roshan kar deti hai, tumhari aankhon mein dekhoon toh waqt kuch pal ke liye thehar sa jaata hai, aur tumhari awaaz sunte hi dil ko apne ghar jaisa sukoon milta hai. Tum itni khoobsurat ho ki tumhari tasveer dekh kar bhi chehre par muskaan aa jaati hai, lekin tumhari asli khoobsurti tumhare pyaare dil, tumhari saadgi, tumhari samajh aur sabka khayal rakhne ke tareeke mein hai. Tum jis tarah apne sapnon ke liye mehnat karti ho, apni baaton se mujhe samajhti ho aur meri chhoti-chhoti khushiyon ko apna bana leti ho—yeh sab mujhe tum par har roz aur zyada fakhr karata hai.

Meri Eiraa, tumhare saath ek aam sa din bhi yaadgaar ban jaata hai. Tumse baat karna, tumhari hansi sunna, tumhari shararatein dekhna, ya bas tumhare paas chup-chaap rehna—har pal mere liye khaas hai. Tum meri pyaari si aadat ho, meri subah ka pehla khayal aur raat ka aakhri ehsaas. Tumhare saath main apna asli roop ho sakta hoon; tum mujhe sunti ho, samajhti ho, aur apne pyaar se behtar banne ki himmat deti ho. Main chahta hoon ki tum hamesha apne aap ko utna hi khoobsurat, khaas aur kaabil samjho jitni tum meri nazron mein ho. Tumhe kisi aur jaisa hone ki zaroorat nahi—tum jaisi ho, bilkul waisi hi lajawab ho.

Teri hansi se roshan meri har shaam ho jaaye,
tera haath mere haath mein ho, toh har raasta aasaan ho jaaye.
Tum sirf khoobsurat lamha nahi, meri har dua ka jawaab ho—
meri duniya ke har pyaare khwaab mein, bas tumhara hi naam ho.

Main tumse sirf tumhari khoobsurti ki wajah se nahi, balki tumhari poori rooh se pyaar karta hoon—tumhari achchai, tumhari himmat, tumhari masoomiyat, tumhari hasi aur tumhari har chhoti si baat se. Tumhari khushi mere liye bahut maayne rakhti hai, aur main chahta hoon ki tumhe hamesha mehsoos ho ki tum kitni pyaari ho aur tumhara saath mere liye kitna anmol hai. Zindagi ke har naye din mein tumhare saath aur khoobsurat yaadein banana chahta hoon. Mera haath thaam kar chalogi na, meri jaan? Tum meri pasand ho, meri dua ho, meri sabse pyaari si duniya ho—aur tumse pyaar karna mere dil ka sabse khoobsurat faisla hai.
